# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they help developers migrate away from DAWA or pick
the right post-DAWA service.

Good contributions include:

- verified endpoint mappings;
- examples in more languages;
- DAR/Datafordeler examples for lookup, BFE/BBR, GraphQL, events, WFS, and
  downloads;
- migration notes from real DAWA integrations;
- clearer error handling and auth guidance;
- links to official SDFI/Dataforsyningen documentation.

Please keep these rules:

- Do not commit tokens, credentials, or customer data.
- Verify links before citing them.
- Prefer official sources over blog posts or vendor pages.
- Be explicit when a DAWA feature needs DAR/Datafordeler, Adressevælger, the
  planned Adressevask replacement, events, downloads, WFS/OGC, or another
  service instead of GSearch.
- Do not describe GSearch as Google Search.
